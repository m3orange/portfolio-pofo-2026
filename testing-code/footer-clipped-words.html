Founding Product Designer

San Francisco, CA

About the Role

You'll build design from the ground-up at both Pool the product and Pool the company. You’ll shape the customer experience end-to-end: onboarding, identity flows, sending and managing money, and all the edge cases in between that make payments hard. This is a role for someone who loves shipping, details, and making complex financial concepts feel simple.

In this role, you’ll lead product design across discovery, prototyping, user research, and high-fidelity execution. This role is an excellent fit for someone who is opinionated and collaborative, and comfortable making calls with incomplete data. This role isn't a great fit for those seeking extensive mentorship or a manager role.

Who you are

  • You're someone who cares deeply about impact and the customers you serve. You want real ownership over what is shipped. You've worked on a product people use weekly (or daily) and are not afraid of regulation and compliance.
  • You love getting it right, have 3-8 years of product design experience, and have strong opinions about typography, interaction design, brand design and product narratives. You love working with engineering teams to bring your designs to life.
  • You've used Figma professionally and you're actively experimenting with AI in your design workflow. It could be for prototyping, generative exploration, or asset creation. We're an AI-first team with a "pick your own AI stack" budget, and we see AI as a creative multiplier, not a threat.
  • You think in systems, not screens. You'll create the foundational components, define the guidelines, and evolve the system as our product and team grow. You know a good system is a living thing, not a rigid spec, and it will evolve it alongside the product.
  • You want to be part of a focused, high-performing team, and you're available to work in-person three days a week in San Francisco (relocation is possible!)

Why Pool?

We're more connected than ever, yet we're often stuck with single-player financial tools. Pool is building a modern financial account that is multiplayer from day one. It should be easier to share, co-own and spend money together. That's why Pool exists.

We care as much about the team we build as the product we ship, and know that quality compounds for both. We want to work with others who are rigorous, curious and kind.

We're payments people, and we love coming to our SF office to brainstorm, collaborate, and build together. If you're someone who is energized by the earliest version of a company and product, we’d love to meet you.

How to apply

Send an email to hello@poolmoney.com with your Linkedin profile or a resume attached as PDF. Please answer the following in plain text:

  • Tell us about yourself and your background. 100 words or less.
  • Include a link to your portfolio or 2–3 case studies.
  • Tell us what excites you about Pool and consumer payments software, along with relevant payments experience if you have any. We want to work with people who are excited to use the product they’re building. 100 words or less.
  • Tell us about something you’ve read recently and would recommend to us – we want to learn about you as a person and what interests you. 100 words or less.
