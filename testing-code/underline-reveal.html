Field Notes · № 4

aWhat does the studio actually make?

Identity systems, editorial websites and the occasional typeface. We take four projects a year and finish all four — the constraint is the offer.

bHow does an engagement begin?

With a two-week discovery sprint: interviews, an audit, and a written point of view. If we're wrong for each other, you keep the document and we part as friends.

cDo you work with early-stage teams?

Two of our four annual slots are reserved for pre-seed and seed companies at a reduced rate. Bring conviction; the deck can be ugly.

dWhere are you based?

Copenhagen and Toronto, and the six hours of overlap between them. Everything ships from a shared repository and a long-running phone call.
